hackathon, personal safety

click the baklava

What if asking for help didn't look like asking for help? That was the question behind this safety app. You set a hidden tap sequence that only you know, and when you use it, a trusted contact silently gets your live location.

We built it with a team at a hackathon, in React and TypeScript, racing the clock the whole way.

tech for good award

machine learning, computer vision

ai dog & cat breed identifier

Everyone has pointed at a dog and wondered what breed it is. We turned that question into something more useful: upload a photo, and our computer vision model identifies the breed, then surfaces health, diet, and care guidance for that specific animal.

It's a team project, and we're still building it.
